HillTop Haven

4th Floor T22

HillTop is a place that is air conditioned and mostly silent. It is a excellant place to revise and meet up with groupmates.

There consistent power plugs to charge your devices and make sure it is ready for class and group work.

Printers

T22 Level 3, Library

The closest place to get your printing done is at T22 third floor near the elevator. 4 cents for black and white, 45 cents for colored. Library although further, it is good to use it when you are studying using the library rooms.
Operation times are 7:30am to 7:00pm, good range if you have early classes or have late dismissal timings.

Hidden Treasures Close By!

Food Court 6

Japanese Stall has extremely nice food at an acceptable price of $5.50 and below! (Not including add-ons like eggs or cheese).

Yong Tau Foo gives generous servings and has a varied selection of sides for around less than $5.

Creamy Duck sells various waffles with ice cream, fruits or syrups like chocolate and peanut butter.

Food Court 5

Multiple well-known fast food options are here! They have KFC and Subway! Good if you want a sweet cookie or sandwich to snack on or eat a party meal with your classmates!
